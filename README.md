# AI Business Command Center

> A recruiter-facing business intelligence project designed to turn business signals into decisions.

## Live Demo
https://ai-business-command-center-eosin.vercel.app/

## What it demonstrates
- Executive KPI command center
- Revenue and profit trajectory analysis
- Regional and category performance
- Root-cause decomposition
- AI-assisted executive narrative
- Prioritized business decision queue
- Interactive discount what-if simulator
- Responsive premium dashboard experience

## Business question
What if a dashboard did more than report what happened — what if it also helped explain why it happened and what a business could investigate or do next?

## Project positioning
The project intentionally focuses on business reasoning rather than unnecessary model complexity. Analytical findings are presented first, with an AI-assisted narrative layer translating validated metrics into concise business language.

## Suggested interview explanation
“I designed the project around moving from descriptive analytics toward decision intelligence. The dashboard surfaces the key business metrics, decomposes performance changes into likely contributors, and then translates those validated findings into plain-English recommendations. I kept the AI layer constrained to the analytical outputs so it would support — rather than replace — the underlying business analysis.”
