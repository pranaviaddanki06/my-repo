Decision Intelligence

AI Insight Studio

Evidence-led insight cards: What happened → why it matters → why → recommendation → action.

Explainable · simulated
01 · WHAT HAPPENED

Retention improved, but the gain is uneven across customer cohorts.

30-day retention reached 68.4%, while newer low-workflow cohorts remain materially more fragile.

02 · WHY IT MATTERS

Small retention changes compound through customer lifetime value.

At current scale, a modeled +5 point retention improvement could materially increase recurring revenue without proportional acquisition spend.

03 · EVIDENCE

Workflow breadth is the strongest behavioral signal in this simulation.

Customers using three or more workflows show stronger repeat activity than single-workflow users.

04 · RECOMMENDATION

Launch an activation play around the first 14 days.

Prioritize industry-specific onboarding, workflow nudges and proactive outreach for high-value customers showing early inactivity.

05 · EXPECTED IMPACT

Scenario range: ₹42L–₹68L annualized revenue opportunity.

This is a modeled scenario, not a forecast or company fact. Validate through controlled experimentation.
