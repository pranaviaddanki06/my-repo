From signal → evidence → action

Structured insight writing, with the analytical evidence kept visible.

Workflow depth is the first intervention to investigate.

What happened: 2,146 accounts are modeled at risk. Evidence: risk is concentrated among lower multi-feature usage. Why it matters: retaining an existing active account can be more directly testable than broad acquisition. Next action: run a targeted activation experiment and measure 30D retention.

Overdue exposure should become an operational queue, not just a KPI.

Evidence: ₹24.6L modeled overdue exposure, with concentration in a smaller set of high-value accounts. Next action: prioritize collection sequences by value × overdue days.

Bundle adoption is a testable growth hypothesis.

Evidence: invoice + inventory + reminders is the strongest simulated workflow association. Next action: test guided onboarding against a control group.

Methodology & provenance

How this case study is built

Clear boundaries between evidence, modeling and interpretation.

01 · Product context

The analytical questions are framed around MSME billing, accounting, inventory, payments, reminders, reports and operational workflows associated with a business platform like Vyapar.

02 · Modeled data

The displayed business metrics are synthetic/model inputs created for a portfolio demonstration. They are not extracted from Vyapar internal systems.

03 · Analytics

The interface demonstrates segmentation, retention thinking, feature adoption, anomaly detection concepts, opportunity sizing and scenario sensitivity.

04 · Decision layer

Each dashboard layer ends with an investigation, action queue or testable hypothesis rather than stopping at a visualization.

05 · Statistical caution

Associations are described as signals to investigate. Simulated scenario values are directional and should not be interpreted as causal estimates or company forecasts.

06 · Recruiter takeaway

The project is designed to show SQL/data-model thinking, KPI design, customer/product analytics, business reasoning, experimentation and communication—not claim access to proprietary data.

Portfolio disclosure: Vyapar Growth Intelligence is a simulated analytics product built by Pranavi Addanki. Metrics and customer records shown here are synthetic/model inputs. The experience is for portfolio and demonstration purposes and must not be interpreted as Vyapar internal performance.
