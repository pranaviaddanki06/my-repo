# Vyapar Growth Intelligence Engine

A recruiter-facing Product & Data Analytics portfolio case study built around an MSME SaaS product.

## What this demonstrates
- SQL-style product data modeling
- Customer health scoring and churn-risk analysis
- Retention and cohort analysis
- Feature adoption intelligence
- MSME segmentation
- Voice-of-customer analysis
- Experiment design and statistical reasoning
- Executive BI storytelling

## Important
This is a portfolio simulation. Product metrics shown in the interface are synthetic and are **not Vyapar internal data**. Public review themes, when referenced, are treated as a separate external signal.

## Stack
HTML/CSS/JavaScript · Chart.js · SQL concepts · Python/ML methodology · Power BI concepts

## Case-study question
How could a product analytics team turn behavioral data into earlier retention signals and clearer growth investigations?

## Recruiter takeaway
The project is designed to show the full analytical workflow: define the business question → structure data → analyze behavior → identify signals → test hypotheses → communicate decisions.
